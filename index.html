One Percent

What would you charge one person, once?

Answer that, and the arithmetic does something uncomfortable: multiply it by fifty thousand people. This is a half-hour call about the number you just produced, and the first customer standing behind it.

$7 a session. Paid before the call. The work is worth a hundred dollars an hour and that is what it is priced at everywhere else. Seven is what is asked here. The difference is deliberate and it is not a discount you have to qualify for.

What happens

  1. Thirty minutes, in your browser. Nothing to install, nothing to dial, no number and no app. You press a button on your own order page and it starts.
  2. Eight questions, in order. What you need, what you can do, what you charge, the arithmetic run out loud on your own number, who the first paying customer is, what is standing in the way, and what can be arranged about that one thing.
  3. One action, with a name against it. Not five good ideas. One thing, and who is doing it. A session that ends in a list ends in nothing.

Those eight questions are written down and published in full — read the method before you pay. Nothing is held back for the session and nobody is screened for fitness. The only question is whether seven dollars is worth answering them and hearing what I make of your answers.

It is an automated assistant, not a person

It says so in its first sentence, and it says the session is recorded. A person reads everything it takes down.

It will not take an account of what has been done to you. If you start, it says one line and takes you back to the question rather than hanging up — you have paid for the half hour. That is deliberate. This is not therapy and a machine is not the listener.

It gives no advice, promises nothing, and decides nothing about you. It asks, it writes down what you said in your own words, and a person reads it.

Before you pay

No refunds.
The work happens the moment the session runs. If it drops, open your link and start it again — the seven buys one completed session, not one attempt. What you are paying for is stated above and the method is published in full, so there is nothing to find out afterwards.
The figure is arithmetic, not a forecast.
Fifty thousand people at your rate is a target with your own number under it. It is not income, it is not owed to you, and nobody has reached it.
This is not therapy, and it is not a job board.
It is one conversation about what you would have to do, with somebody who has already done the arithmetic on themselves.
Nothing you say is published without you asking for it.
Notes exist for the work. Publication is a separate question, asked later, and saying no to it costs you nothing.
No account needed.
You do not have to be in Skills Economy, or anywhere else. Anyone can book.

Book a session

Wise from anywhere, or an Amazon gift card if you have no bank account — one can be bought with cash in a shop.

Pay for a session

You are not asked for your name, your email address or where you are. You enter a gift card code and get a link back. When the payment is matched, that link is where your session opens.
