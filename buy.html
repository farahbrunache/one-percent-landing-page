Pay for a session

Seven dollars, by gift card. No account, no card number, no email address. You are not asked for your name and you are not asked where you are.

Buy a gift card — Amazon, Apple, or any of the others below — and enter its code here. One can be bought with cash in a shop if you have no card and no bank account.

You get a link back. Keep it. When the card is checked, that link is where the phone number and your access code appear.

In US dollars. A session is $7. Anything over that is not refunded.
Dashes and spaces do not matter.

Lost your link?

Enter the same card code and you get a new one. Nothing else can find your order, because nothing else about you was recorded.

There are no refunds. What you are paying for is set out on the main page, and the eight questions are published in full, so there is nothing to find out afterwards.

Your card code is destroyed once it has been checked. Nothing that identifies you is stored at any point.
